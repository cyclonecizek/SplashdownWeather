Sources

    Wind. When NBM is the only wind source switched on, the mean and 99th percentile come straight from NBM's own quantile-mapped wind distribution. Otherwise, each hour, every member's 10 m wind at the site is pooled, with each source's weight split across its members. Super GO needs the ensemble mean at or below 7.25 kt and the 99th percentile at or below 10.0 kt; GO needs at or below 7.75 kt and 11.5 kt; anything else is NO-GO. A window takes its worst hour.

    Ceiling and visibility sources. Direct guidance comes from the hi-res models' own ceiling and visibility, REFS and HREF ensemble probabilities, NBM probabilities, and GFS MOS and LAMP. Where none of these covers a window (mostly beyond about 3 days), the ceiling row falls back to a stand-in from the global ensembles: a member counts when the cloud base of lifted surface air (about 125 m per °C of dew point depression) is below 500 ft and low cloud cover is at least 70% (when the model provides it). Stand-in cells are hatched.

    Marine layer setup (awareness only, not a constraint) finds the lowest temperature inversion above each site in every member that has a temperature profile: HRRR at 25 mb steps from 1000 to 850 mb, and the global ensembles at the pressure levels Open-Meteo provides. A member counts when the inversion base is at or below 500 m, it warms at least 3 °C across the inversion, and the air under it is moist (2 m dew point depression of 2.5 °C or less, or 1000 mb humidity of 90% or more). The chart shows the median inversion base; limits are in config.yaml.

    Dew point depression (2 m temperature minus dew point, °C) is shown for awareness of fog and low visibility potential and does not count toward GO/NO-GO. It is the weighted median across every model and MOS member; a window shows its lowest hourly median. Yellow is within 3 °C, red within 1 °C.

    Weather. Each value is the chance a member meets the condition at some hour in the window, weight-averaged across sources and rounded to 5%. Precipitation counts anywhere within 10 nm: 1 km reflectivity of 20 dBZ or more for the hi-res models, 0.01 in/hr at the site or a 10 nm ring for the global ensembles, and NBM's 1-hour precipitation probability. Lightning counts within 10 nm and uses only explicit lightning output: the hi-res lightning field and NBM thunderstorm probability. Ceiling below 500 ft and visibility below 1 statute mile are at the site, from the hi-res models and NBM probabilities (at NBM's nearest thresholds, listed under Sources), plus GFS MOS and LAMP at the nearest near-sea-level station with guidance (listed under Sources). MOS gives a best-category forecast rather than a probability, so each of its two latest runs counts as one member. "Any weather" counts a member once if it meets any checked condition, and is never below the lightning, ceiling or visibility rows, which only a few sources provide.
